## Arka Dutta

M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence at IIT Gandhinagar, in the CVIG Lab.

My research is on surgical video understanding: monocular depth estimation to
recover scene geometry, and procedure planning on top of it. Part of a
DST-funded project with NTU Singapore's Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine,
structured as independently evaluated work packages.

Outside the thesis I build systems that get deployed and stay deployed:
retrieval-augmented assistants, language identification models trained from
scratch, and quantitative modelling pipelines.

### Selected work

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[Research Park assistant](https://chatbot-wheat-eight-58.vercel.app)** | Retrieval-augmented assistant for the IIT Gandhinagar Research Park, answering visitor questions on collaboration, research infrastructure and incubation. | **[Multimodal price prediction](https://github.com/Arka-Dutta-28/ecommerce-product-price-prediction)** | Amazon ML Hackathon 2025. Qwen summarisation over product text with BERT and ResNet embeddings, fused for price regression. |

### Reproduction work

Reproduced Supplementary Figure 2 of TAPIR / PSI-AVA (Valderrama et al., MICCAI
2022) from the published annotations. 53 of 55 per-class keyframe counts match
the figure exactly; the two that differ are off by one keyframe each, out of
73,618.

### How these repositories are organised

Numbers quoted in a README are traceable to the log or results file they came
from, and constraints are stated rather than omitted. Repositories are private
while the underlying work is unpublished. Completed coursework and finished
analyses are archived rather than deleted, each with a note saying what it was.
